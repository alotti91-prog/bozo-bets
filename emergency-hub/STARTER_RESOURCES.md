# Starter resources

No documents are bundled with the app: they belong to their publishers, and you should get current editions
from the source. Download these **while you're online**, then import them (Library → Choose files, or
`python3 hub.py import ~/Downloads/emergency --category "First Aid"`).

These are real, widely used publications, but I couldn't open the publishers' sites from the environment this
was built in, so **titles, links and terms weren't re-checked**. Search the publisher's site for the title,
check the licence or terms before sharing copies, and prefer the newest edition.

| Category | Document | Publisher | Licence / terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Family Preparedness | *Are You Ready? An In-Depth Guide to Citizen Preparedness* | FEMA (fema.gov / ready.gov) | US government work, public domain |
| Family Preparedness, Power Outages | Ready.gov pages on Build a Kit, Make a Plan, Power Outages (save each page as PDF from your browser) | FEMA / Ready.gov | US government work, public domain |
| Water and Sanitation | "Making Water Safe in an Emergency" and related emergency water pages (save as PDF) | US CDC (cdc.gov) | US government work, public domain |
| Food and Preservation | "Keeping Food Safe During an Emergency" | USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service (fsis.usda.gov) | US government work, public domain |
| Food and Preservation | *Complete Guide to Home Canning* | USDA, via the National Center for Home Food Preservation (nchfp.uga.edu) | US government work, public domain |

| First Aid | Your national Red Cross / Red Crescent / St John first aid guide or app content | your national society | Copyrighted: personal use only |

| Gardening | Planting calendars and vegetable guides from your local agricultural extension service | e.g. a state university extension | Usually free; check terms |
| Home Repairs | Your utility companies' guides to shutoffs, gas leaks and outages; your appliances' manuals | your utilities / manufacturers | Personal use |

## Tips

- **Local beats general.** Your country's emergency agency, your local health department, and your water and
  power utilities have advice for your area.
- **Medical:** for anything involving doses or treatments, the source to trust is a current, authoritative
  manual or a professional, never AI text. Keep a printed first aid manual as well.
- **Scanned PDFs** have no text to search. Run OCR on them first (see README troubleshooting).
- **Web pages:** "Print → Save as PDF" in your browser makes an importable copy. Note the date you saved it.
- Don't import things you don't have the right to keep, and don't redistribute copyrighted material.
